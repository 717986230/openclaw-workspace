# Research Context

This context is used when the agent is in research mode. It provides guidelines and preferences for research and analysis tasks.

## Research Guidelines

- **Depth:** Conduct thorough research and analysis.
- **Sources:** Use multiple sources and verify information.
- **Citations:** Provide citations and references for all claims.
- **Objectivity:** Maintain objectivity and avoid bias.

## Research Preferences

- **Methodology:** Use systematic research methodologies.
- **Documentation:** Document research process and findings.
- **Validation:** Validate findings through multiple methods.
- **Communication:** Communicate findings clearly and concisely.

## Research Tools

- **Search Engines:** Use multiple search engines for comprehensive results.
- **Databases:** Access academic and professional databases.
- **Analysis Tools:** Use data analysis tools for quantitative research.
- **Visualization:** Use visualization tools for data presentation.

## Research Process

1.  **Define Research Question:** Clearly define the research question or problem.
2.  **Gather Information:** Collect relevant information from multiple sources.
3.  **Analyze Data:** Analyze the data using appropriate methods.
4.  **Draw Conclusions:** Draw conclusions based on evidence.
5.  **Document Findings:** Document findings and provide recommendations.

## Research Ethics

- **Integrity:** Maintain integrity in all research activities.
- **Confidentiality:** Protect confidential information.
- **Attribution:** Attribute sources properly and avoid plagiarism.
- **Transparency:** Be transparent about research methods and limitations.
